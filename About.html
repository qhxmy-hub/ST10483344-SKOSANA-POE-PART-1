Themba Givers is a non profit organization that provides food parcels to the unfortunate and pension earners. Themba Givers also provides weekly feeding schemes to local communities and try to donate clothes to children in the community based on the donations by donators and whether they hosting a food drive or clothing drive. Members of the community can offer their help by signing up on the website to voluteer to either help dish up for the community, help clean up the community parks, help hand out food parcels to pension earners and the elderly.

Elderly people that require help with their pension fund can get assistance by going to the Enquiries page for further assistance with their pension or finding a proffesional that will help them with claiming their funds without them getting scammed or robbed of their pension fund making their elderly life a harder life than they had anticipated and living off a SASSA grant.
