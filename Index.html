Making A Difference Together

Themba Givers is a non-profit organisation that works with local communities to provide food, clothing and support to people who need it most.

Donate or Volunteer

How You Can Help

Donate

Donate food, clothing or other items to help families and elderly people in our communities.

Volunteer

Give your time by helping with feeding schemes, food drives and community clean-ups.

Support

Help us spread awareness and encourage other people to support our community projects.

Our Goal

Our goal is to bring hope to local communities by helping people access basic necessities and by encouraging community members to work together.
