WORK THAT WE DO IN COMMUNITIES

We organise different community activities that are focused on supporting people and improving local areas.

Food Drives

We collect food donations and distribute food parcels to people and families who need assistance.

Feeding Schemes

Volunteers help prepare and provide meals to members of local communities.

Community Clean-ups

Volunteers help clean public spaces and community parks to create cleaner environments.

Clothing Drives

We collect new and used clothing and distribute it to people who require assistance.
